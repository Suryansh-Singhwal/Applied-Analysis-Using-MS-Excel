## Project Overview

An end-to-end **Ferns N Petals (FNP) sales analysis project** developed using Microsoft Excel. The project transforms raw sales data into business insights through **Power Query, Power Pivot, Data Modeling, PivotTables, and an interactive dashboard**.

## Business Questions & Answers

### 1. Total Revenue

**Question:** What is the overall revenue generated?

**Answer:** Total revenue generated is **₹3,520,984**.

### 2. Average Delivery Time

**Question:** What is the average time taken for orders to be delivered?

**Answer:** The average delivery time is **5.53 days**.

### 3. Monthly Sales Performance

**Question:** How do sales fluctuate across the months of 2023?

**Answer:** **August** generated the highest monthly revenue at **₹737,389**, followed by February at **₹704,509**. January recorded the lowest revenue at **₹95,468**.

## Project Outcome

The project converts raw FNP sales data into an **interactive Excel dashboard** that answers key business questions and provides insights into **revenue, products, customers, cities, occasions, sales trends, and delivery performance**.

The analysis can support better **sales strategy, product planning, occasion-based marketing, customer targeting, and operational decision-making**.
